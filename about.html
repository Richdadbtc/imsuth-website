About IMSUTH

A teaching hospital bringing healthcare delivery, medical learning and research together in service of patients and communities.

Overview

Care, learning and inquiry under one roof.

Imo State University Teaching Hospital, Orlu is a tertiary healthcare institution and clinical learning environment. Its role connects specialist patient care with the education and supervised training of healthcare professionals.

01

Patient care

Delivering respectful, patient-centred healthcare through hospital teams and clinical services.

02

Medical education

Supporting the clinical learning required to prepare future healthcare professionals.

03

Clinical training

Providing supervised practical experience within a working teaching-hospital environment.

04

Research

Creating an environment where evidence and inquiry can contribute to improved care.

05

Community service

Extending the hospital’s public-service role to the wider community it serves.
